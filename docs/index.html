Beat is the slow rhythm your brain is invited to follow, in cycles per second. Lower feels sleepier, higher more alert.

Pitch is the tone that carries the beat. It does not change the tuning, so choose whatever sounds comfortable.

Timbre is the colour of the tone, from a plain sine to a warmer, richer voice.

Pad adds a quiet fifth, octave and sub beneath the tone.

Beat style chooses how the beat is made. Binaural needs headphones. Pulsing works out loud. Wavering sums both tones in the centre. Auto picks for you.

Rainfall sets how hard the rain falls; the Rain slider sets how loud it is.

Sea sets how big the waves are; the Ocean slider sets how loud they are.
